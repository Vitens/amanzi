# Plate aeration

Library name: **Plate Aeration**. Plate / contact aerator with optional recirculated off-gas.

## Place it when

You are using plate aerators rather than a packed tower or cascade.

## Streams

- In/out: product.

## Inputs (UI groups)

- **Design:** plate geometry as in the form.
- **Operational:** RQ, recirculation fraction.
- **Model:** efficiency factor.

## Outputs / design tables

- Gas–liquid equilibrium after multi-pass air interaction.
- Design RQ sweep charts.
- Energy of air handling.
