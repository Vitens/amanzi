# Sand filtration

Library name: **Sand filtration**. Dual-media rapid filter: optional spray aeration plus oxidation and precipitation of iron, manganese, and ammonium.

## Place it when

You are removing iron, manganese, ammonium, methane, or H₂S on a sand/anthracite bed.

## Streams

- In: product.
- Out: product (filtrate); flush (iron-loaded backwash).

## Inputs (UI groups)

- Shared capacities, elevations, filtration geometry (from filtration layer): area, bed, supernatant.
- **Sand filter:** dual media on/off; anthracite height/diameter/porosity; sand height/diameter/porosity (clean and clogged).
- **Model:** suppression flags and removal efficiencies for Fe / NH₄ / Mn.
- **Spray aerator** (optional): fall height, RQ, efficiency.
- **Backwash programme:** steps and rates.

## Outputs / design tables

- Stepwise oxidation / speciation table in design view.
- Headloss (Kozeny–Carman), backwash expansion.
- Energy and sustainability when blowers/pumps apply.
