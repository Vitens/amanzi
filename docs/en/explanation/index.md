# Explanation

These pages are for understanding. They discuss how a treatment train works and how Amanzi calculates it. Steps for doing a task live in the [how-to guides](../how-to/index.md). A first walk through the demo lives in the [tutorials](../tutorials/index.md). Names, fields, and allowed values live in the [reference](../reference/index.md). That split follows the [Diátaxis](https://diataxis.fr/map/) map: tutorial, how-to, reference, explanation.

There is no required order. A useful cycle is to try a tutorial, do a how-to, look up a field, and come here for why the number moved.
