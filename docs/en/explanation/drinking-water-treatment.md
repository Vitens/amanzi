# Drinking-water treatment

Groundwater becomes drinking water through a series of steps. Everyday language; no plant-design course.

## Content to write

- Groundwater already contains dissolved substances (iron, hardness, methane, sometimes PFAS).
- A **treatment train** is a sequence of rooms/machines, each doing one job (aerate, filter, soften, dose, membrane, carbon).
- **Product water** goes toward the tap; **waste** is concentrate or sludge water that cannot be sent as drinking water; **backwash** is wash water from filters.
- Why a drawing of boxes and arrows is a useful model of that train.
- Amanzi’s library is that set of boxes for a typical Dutch groundwater works — not every process on earth.

## Diagrams

- Mermaid: groundwater → aeration/filtration/softening/… → drinking water, with waste/backwash side streams.
