# Reading water quality

What the quality labels mean without assuming chemistry. For Amanzi’s sidebar, not a textbook.

## Content to write

- **pH** — how acidic/alkaline; why plants care (corrosion, calcite, disinfection context in one sentence).
- **Hardness / Ca / Mg** — “lime” in water; why softening exists.
- **Fe, Mn** — stain and turbidity; why sand filters exist.
- **NH₄** — ammonium; often removed biologically/chemically on filters.
- **CH₄ / H₂S / O₂ / N₂** — dissolved gases; why aeration exists.
- **HCO₃, SI, CCPP/TACC₉₀** — calcite / aggressiveness, in one accessible paragraph each.
- **EGV / sc20 / TDS** — how “salty” or conductive the water is.
- **PFAS** — persistent man-made substances, ng/L, grouped as Sum4/Sum20/PEQ; details in [Activated carbon and PFAS](activated-carbon-and-pfas.md).
- These numbers are produced by **PHREEQC** from the analysis you typed on Abstraction — you do not need to run PHREEQC yourself.
