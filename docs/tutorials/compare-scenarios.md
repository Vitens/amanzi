# Compare scenarios

A scenario is one version of the plant. Make a second version, change one design choice, and open the report.

## Content to write

- Scenario tabs sit at the bottom; `+` creates another scenario.
- Copy the demo idea: keep the same layout, change one block or one number so the two tabs are comparable.
- Notes on a scenario (v1.0.5) are for yourself; they do not change the calculation.
- **Calculate project** / report: look at the comparison of quantity, quality, energy, and sustainability across tabs.
- Do not explain how CO₂ is computed here — that is [Quantity, energy, chemicals, CO₂](../explanation/quantity-energy-chemicals-co2.md).
