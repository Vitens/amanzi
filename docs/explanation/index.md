# Explanation

This section is for understanding. The **primer** pages are for people new to drinking-water treatment. The **science** pages are for key users who want the models, assumptions, and literature.

There is no required reading order, but a useful cycle is: try a tutorial → do a how-to → look up a fact → come here for why.

## Rules for this section

- Discuss, compare, and cite. Do not give click-by-click steps.
- Literature belongs here, not in tutorials or how-tos.
- Diagrams: Mermaid process and calculation sketches.
